Home — Facilities

Spaces built for every kind of learner.

Computer and science labs, a skylit library, sports grounds, an amphitheatre, transport and daily care, on an IGBC Platinum green campus in Yelahanka, North Bangalore.

Learning Arts, Sport & Reflection Everyday Care Green School

Learning Spaces

Room to read, test and build.

Computer Labs

Two separate ICT labs, one for the junior school and one for the senior school. Students follow the CBSE Information Technology curriculum and learn to use technology across other subjects.

Library

A bright, airy reading space with natural light from a skylight. It is stocked with age-appropriate books, journals and periodicals across fiction, non-fiction and reference.

Science Labs

Dedicated Physics, Chemistry and Biology laboratories, equipped so students can perform experiments individually as per CBSE guidelines. A qualified laboratory in-charge and the science teacher supervise each session.

Montessori Learning Center

For children aged 3–6. Early childhood professionals guide a multi-age setting where each child progresses individually and works with concrete materials of their choice.

Math Lab

Junior Science & Math Activity Centers use interactive materials, models and toys, so students can see and handle abstract ideas.

Composite Lab

Simple experiments and open-ended exploration connect science and creativity to everyday life.

Resource Center

Trained special educators support every kind of learner, in collaboration with Adhyan Trust and Teachers Collective.

Arts, Sport & Reflection

Room to move and perform.

Sports

A dedicated sports field for basketball, football, cricket, skating and field events, with a structured fitness programme under an experienced physical education instructor. Students are automatically enrolled at the Padukone–Dravid Centre for Sports Excellence.

Amphitheatre

Called the heart of the school. A large performance stage with ascending seats for over 650, modern projection and audio, and internet access. It hosts assemblies and events, and students use the steps to study and meet.

Sanctuary Room

A dedicated space for children to reflect and pray. Sanctuary time reinforces the seven pillars of education advocated by Rev. Dada Vaswani.

Performing & Visual Arts

Drawing, painting, craft, music, dance, theatre and public speaking, under qualified teachers and instructors.

Yoga & Dance Studio

Yoga builds mindfulness and focus. Indian and Western dance build rhythm, confidence and creativity.

Everyday Care

Looked after, all day.

Transport

GPS-enabled buses on routes planned for the closest pick-up and drop-off points. Every bus has a trained driver and a female attendant. Parents can follow routes on the Roorides App.

Infirmary

A qualified nurse on campus, and a tie-up with the nearest hospital for emergencies until parents arrive.

Daycare

Supervised daycare for pre-primary students, with play-based and structured activities.

Pre-Primary Play Area

A colourful, inclusive play area designed for safety, creativity and peer interaction.

Cafeteria

A bright, eco-conscious space designed for health, connection and well-being.

Meal Plan

Optional breakfast at 8:30 AM and lunch at 12:00 PM, with regional, seasonal menus curated with Team Rujuta Diwekar.

Books & Uniform

School books, stationery and uniforms are available on the school premises at the beginning of the academic year.

Green School

Certified IGBC Platinum.

  • Natural light and high-efficiency fixtures, with artificial lighting kept to a minimum
  • Rainwater harvesting cistern and low-flow fixtures
  • A green classroom, where a student ambassador leads energy-saving and recycling efforts
