Home — About SVIS

A school built on truth, service and learning that is rooted in values.

Sadhu Vaswani International School, Bangalore opened its doors in June 2021 on a five-acre green campus at Bettahalasur, Yelahanka — continuing the Mira Movement in Education begun by Sadhu T.L. Vaswani in 1933.

Our story

From a small campus in Dollars Colony to a full K-12 CBSE school in North Bangalore.

Sadhu Vaswani International School, Bangalore began operations in June 2021 with Nursery, Kindergarten I & II, and Grades 1–5. Classes for Grades 6 and 7 commenced in the academic year 2023–24, and the school continues to grow toward the full K-12 span under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi.

It all started modestly — a few children and a small, committed team of educators at a compact campus in Dollars Colony, Bangalore. As the school grew, it moved to a purpose-built five-acre green campus designed to foster values, self-discipline, motivation and academic excellence, delivering education through the K-12 span, with classes currently running from Nursery to Grade 8 as the school grows each year.

The school aims at nurturing every child's inner potential to prepare them for the modern world — combining academic intelligence with character development to prepare students as responsible future citizens. Guided by Sadhu Vaswani's words, "The root of education is reverence. The fruit of education is service," building empathy and service to humanity is an essential part of the SVIS curriculum.

Ninety years of the Mira Movement

SVIS Bangalore is part of the Sadhu Vaswani Educational Institutions across India.

1933

The Mira School for Girls is inaugurated in Pune, laying the foundation of the Mira Movement in Education. After Partition, Pune becomes the headquarters of Sadhu Vaswani's work.

1950

St. Mira's Educational Board is constituted, chaired by Maharishi D.K. Karve.

1962

St. Mira's College is founded by Sadhu Vaswani — the first college in Pune set up exclusively for women.

1987–2012

Sadhu Vaswani International Schools are established across India — Delhi, Navi Mumbai, Hyderabad and Pradhikaran (Pune) — along with a Girls' School in Rajkot, a school in Ahmedabad and Sadhu Vaswani Vidya Mandir in Vadodara.

2004

The Sadhu Vaswani Mission is established in Bangalore.

2015

Little Lamps Kindergarten is founded in Bangalore.

2017

Little Lamps Kindergarten is founded in London.

2019

Bhoomi pooja is performed for the campus of Sadhu Vaswani International School, Bangalore at Bettahalasur.

2021

Classes begin at Sadhu Vaswani International School, Bangalore.

Our inspiration

Two lives of service whose teachings shape every SVIS classroom.

Sadhu T.L. Vaswani Founder · 1879–1966

Educationist, mystic saint, animal-rights activist and writer. He founded the Sadhu Vaswani Mission in 1929 and created the Mira Movement — a philosophy centred on empowering women through education. "The child is the light and life of the nation."

Dada J.P. Vaswani Spiritual Master · 1918–2018

Practical philosopher and accomplished orator who led the Mission until his passing on 12 July 2018. He expanded the Mira Movement worldwide, blending progressive education with character-building. "The children of today are the builders of a brave, new world."

Sadhu Vaswani Mission Since 1929

A non-sectarian, non-profit organisation believing in the unity of all religions and honouring figures of all faiths — serving through spiritual growth and humanitarian work, regardless of caste, creed or colour.

Education as "a journey of the Spirit" — developing the Head, the Heart and the Hand.
