The SVIS Story

At SVIS, we inspire curiosity, creativity, and innovation. Guided by truth, respect, and excellence, we empower every child to make a meaningful impact with integrity and purpose.

“The root of education is reverence. The fruit of education is service.”

SVIS opened in June 2021 at Bettahalasur, Yelahanka, continuing the Mira Movement in Education begun by Sadhu T.L. Vaswani in 1933.

Academics

One journey, four stages.

Select a stage to see what your child learns.

Montessori & Early Years

Each day begins with songs, stories, art, and theme-based activities that make learning joyful and meaningful. Children explore through hands-on experiences that build curiosity and imagination while nurturing social and emotional growth.

Know more

Primary School

Our syllabus is aligned with the CBSE curriculum. Trained, experienced, and committed faculty work to ensure an engaging, interactive, and vibrant learning environment. A usual day includes academic sessions, music, dance, sport, and other extracurricular activities to keep students eager to come back for more every day.

Know more

Middle School

The curriculum builds strong conceptual foundations while encouraging practical application. Students explore English, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, and Computer Applications, along with Art, Music, and Physical Education. A balanced blend of academics, projects, and digital learning nurtures curiosity, creativity, and independent thinking.

Know more

Secondary School

The program prepares students for the CBSE Board Examinations through a focused yet holistic approach. Emphasis is placed on academic excellence, analytical thinking, and value-based learning. Through career awareness, collaboration, and community engagement, students grow into confident, responsible, and future-ready individuals.
