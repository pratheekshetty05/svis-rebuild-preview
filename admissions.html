Admissions open · 2026–27 · Montessori to Grade 8

Admissions to SVIS Bangalore — transparent, supportive, stress-free.

We welcome students from diverse backgrounds and admit through the year based on vacancies. The admission process begins each November for the following academic year.

Five steps to joining SVIS

From registration to a confirmed seat — usually within days, not weeks.

01

Register your details

Complete the registration form online via the website, or collect a form from the school office at Bettahalasur.

02

Admission team coordination

Our admission team connects with you to guide you through the next steps and answer questions.

03

Student assessment

An age-appropriate on-campus assessment. KG and Grade I: verbal interaction covering perceptual, communication, motor and analytical skills. Grades II–IX: short written test on grade-appropriate content — English and Math for Grade II; English, Math, Science plus chosen Second Language for Grades III–IX.

04

Parent interaction

After the assessment, a session with the academic team / Head of School so we can get to know your family and your child better.

05

Confirmation of admission

If selected, an admission confirmation email is sent within 5 working days. The admission letter is issued and parents must confirm within 7 days to secure the seat.

The kindergarten programme at SVIS is a three-year curriculum — Nursery, LKG (K-I) and UKG (K-II). Mid-year admissions are handled case-by-case, subject to seat availability.

Documents checklist

To be submitted to the admission office at the time of admission.

  • Four passport-size colour photographs of the child, and two each for parents / guardians.
  • Photocopy of the child's birth certificate (original shown for verification).
  • Photocopies of Aadhaar cards for the child and both parents / guardians.
  • Original Transfer Certificate (from Grade 2 onwards).
  • Mark sheet from the previous school (Grade 2 onwards).
  • Medical reports, if there are special conditions that need attention.
  • Additional address proof, if the address differs from the Aadhaar record.
  • Legal documents (custody / guardianship orders), if applicable.

Practical details

Timings, languages, meals, transport and fees — the everyday facts.

School timings

Monday – Friday

  • Kindergarten & Nursery: 8:00 AM – 12:00 Noon
  • Grade I and above: 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • School office: 8:00 AM – 3:30 PM, plus the 2nd and 4th Saturdays 8:00 AM – 12:00 Noon
Languages

Second-language policy

English is the medium of instruction. Hindi and Kannada are offered as second languages; Kannada is mandatory as per NEP, with details varying by grade level.

Meals & transport

Opt-in services

Nutritious breakfast (8:30 AM) and lunch (12:00 PM) from a hygienic cafeteria — optional, at extra cost. GPS-enabled buses with trained drivers and a female attendant on every route.

Fees

Clear and transparent

Fee details for every grade are shared on request. Fees are payable online or offline via the school's parent portal (MCB). Textbooks and notebooks are provided by the school.
